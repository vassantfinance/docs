After 1.5 years of product conceptualization, market research, and product optimizations, we finally did it. **Vassant is live on both iOS and Android.**

Of course, the journey was not a straight line.

Like any startup, there were pivots along the way. Pivots do not equal uncertainty though, they simply signal that a team, an organization, or any moving force recognizes something new and gears itself towards a direction that solves a problem or blocker.

{/* truncate */}

After all the deliberation, we came to a conclusion about what we stood for: Vassant is a platform to streamline wealth creation, built on high-conviction decision-making principles. 

On our platform, we provide you with simple yet vital tools to take control of your own capital allocation.

We started Vassant based on the assumptions that novice investors were looking for direction to get started on their investing journeys and that experienced investors wanted a centralized platform to templatize and organize their personalized investment strategy. Then we did some research and came across a 2025 study that found the median retail investor spends just [**6 minutes**](https://www.investopedia.com/investors-tend-to-be-impulse-buyers-11745450) conducting internet-based research before making a stock trade.

Holding periods have collapsed in sync: U.S. shares were held for an average of [8 years](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2021/12/long-term-investing-decline/) in the late 1950s, but by June 2020, it was [under 6 months](https://www.reuters.com/article/markets/stocks/buy-sell-repeat-no-room-for-hold-in-whipsawing-markets-idUSKBN24Z101/).

This performance gap highlights a systemic flaw in current investment workflows: **speed is being prioritized over conviction, confidence, and strategy.**

At Vassant, we're working hard to tackle these challenges by building a product that provides investors with a streamlined approach to making more confident investing and capital allocation decisions.

This is just the start. As we continue to roll out Vassant, we will continue improving on our product features and experience.

We know there are "app killers" out there. 

We know fintech is saturated with a litany of platforms. 

We know distribution is the hardest part of all of this. 

But we're not stopping because the world of finance and investing is changing so rapidly, less wealth is being created for younger generations, and negative noise is causing too much rethink and hesitation when allocating capital.

We don't just want to be another app, we want to help shift mindsets towards smarter, independent personal finance practices.

To our early supporters, thank you.

Until next time, 
Miles
