# Upgrading

**Connecting Accounts:** When upgrading to paid, users gain the ability to connect one or more financial institutions to sync investment allocations from multiple accounts. Users are not forced to sync an account when upgrading. To first connect an account, users may go to the sidebar located at the top left of the screen, press “Account Information”, scroll down, then press “Connect your brokerage account.” Users shall follow the prompts to link an account with Vassant. Users may connect more than one financial institution, up to 5 maximum. Additional requests to connect more should be sent to support@vassantfinance.com.

**Syncing Accounts:** To then sync an account, users may go to the “Model” tab → select desired investment account card → “Connect account”. A prompt to match accounts will follow. Users may only match 1 synced account with 1 allocations account. All accounts in the “Yearly Investing Allocations” section not matched to an account while in the Premium subscription remain in “Manual” mode and are not affected.

- **Note**: If an investment account is synced in the "Invest" section, the corresponding account will also be synced in the "Model" section. You may individually unsync either. Unsyncing one will not unsync the other.

- **Data Migration During Sync:** When connected, no data from previously manual contributions will be lost. Starting from the time of connection and sync, that account will update with net cash inflows. Contributions made before syncing the account to Vassant will not update.
  - You may add “Manual” cash inflow contributions to already established “Synced” accounts. The synced amount is always the source of truth for the connected account. A manual entry never overwrites it. A manual cash deposit amount on a synced account counts as "held elsewhere" and is added on top. It stays put through syncs. You edit that one manual amount to change it. New entries don't keep stacking up.
  - *Note:* Vassant does not pull previous contributions made before the time of connection. If tracking calendar year contributions, ensure you manually add any cash deposits so that the cost basis is equal to your true deposit amount before connection.
  - **Example:** A user individually deposits $1000 into their Roth IRA before joining Vassant. This user established an account with Vassant and manually enters a $1000 deposit to the Roth IRA in Vassant. Then, they upgrade to VassantPRO and link their account to Vassant via Plaid. The $1000 manual contribution stays in Vassant (we do not pick up contributions made before connection), and now all future deposits will be automatically tracked in the "Allocations" tab for the Roth IRA. The user may add mannual contributions at any time while synced, even if made earlier on in the calendar year.
 
- **Sync Frequency**: Vassant checks for new data every time you open the Model tab and whenever your brokerage reports changes. Brokerages usually send updates about once a day, so recent cash contributions can take up to a day to appear.

**Unsyncing:** Synced investment accounts may be unsynced at any time inside the account card in the "Allocations" subtab. The progress automatically converts to “Manual Mode”, and all data is maintained from the last available sync. All future contributions must be made via manual contributions. The account may be resynced at any time.

**Tracking Activity History:** At any time, you may track the activity history of deposits and synced allocations in the "View activity history" button on the investment account card in "Model." Contributions will be distinguished between manual and synced.
