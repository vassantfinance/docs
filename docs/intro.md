# Welcome

Hey, glad you're here.

Vassant is built for people who want to actually understand their money, not just hand it off and hope for the best. No jargon walls, no get-rich-quick energy. Just a clearer way to think about where your money goes and why.

## Where to start

Not sure where to click first? Here's the short version:

- **[Discover](./Discover/Knowledge%20Base.md)** - Get the lay of the land. Browse the knowledge base and learn how Vassant thinks about investing.
- **[Model](./Model/Budget/Initial%20Setup.md)** - Set up your budget and allocations. This is where you tell Vassant what you're working with.
- **[Invest](./Invest/General%20Use.md)** - Once your model is dialed in, this is where the actual investing happens.

## A heads up

Vassant is a long-haul tool. It's not here to help you time the market or chase the next hot ticker. It's here to help you build something that lasts...slowly, on purpose.

If you ever get stuck, the [blog](/blog) has more context on the thinking behind all this. Take your time and poke around.
